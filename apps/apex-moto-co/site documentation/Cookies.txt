# Cookie Policy

**Last updated: 25 August 2026**

This Cookie Policy explains how Apex Moto & Co (“Apex Moto & Co”, “we”, “us” or “our”) uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website.

By using our website, you may encounter cookies that are necessary for the website to function, as well as cookies used to understand website usage and improve your experience.

## 1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit a website.

They allow websites to recognise your device and remember certain information about your visit.

Cookies can be used for a number of purposes, including making websites function correctly, remembering preferences, analysing website traffic and supporting marketing activities.

## 2. How We Use Cookies

Apex Moto & Co may use cookies and similar technologies to:

* Make our website function correctly
* Keep the website secure
* Remember your preferences
* Understand how visitors use our website
* Monitor website performance
* Improve our website and services
* Measure the effectiveness of marketing
* Support relevant advertising, where applicable

## 3. Types of Cookies We May Use

### Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are required for the website to operate properly.

They may be used for things such as:

* Website security
* Page navigation
* Forms
* Shopping or booking functionality
* Remembering cookie preferences

These cookies generally cannot be disabled through our website's cookie settings because the website may not function correctly without them.

### Analytics Cookies

Analytics cookies help us understand how visitors use our website.

They may collect information such as:

* Pages visited
* Time spent on the website
* How visitors arrived at the website
* Device and browser information
* General geographic information
* Website interactions

Where required by law, we will ask for your consent before placing analytics cookies on your device.

### Functional Cookies

Functional cookies allow the website to remember choices you make and provide enhanced functionality.

For example, they may remember preferences or settings you have previously selected.

### Marketing Cookies

Marketing cookies may be used to understand browsing activity and measure or personalise advertising.

These may be provided by third-party services such as social media or advertising platforms.

We will seek your consent before using non-essential marketing cookies where required by applicable law.

## 4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies may be placed by third-party services that appear on or are connected to our website.

These may include services used for:

* Website analytics
* Payment processing
* Event registration
* Embedded social media
* Video content
* Advertising
* Marketing

Third-party providers may use cookies in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Apex Moto & Co does not control the cookies placed by third-party websites or services.

## 5. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

Where required, our website may provide a cookie banner or consent management tool allowing you to:

* Accept all cookies
* Reject non-essential cookies
* Manage individual cookie categories
* Change your preferences

You can also control or delete cookies through your browser settings.

Please be aware that disabling certain cookies may affect how the website operates.

## 6. Cookies and Your Privacy

Cookies may involve the collection or processing of information that could be considered personal data.

Where this occurs, we will handle that information in accordance with our **Privacy Policy** and applicable UK data protection legislation.

## 7. How Long Cookies Remain on Your Device

Some cookies are **session cookies**, meaning they are deleted when you close your browser.

Other cookies are **persistent cookies**, meaning they remain on your device for a specified period or until you delete them.

The length of time a cookie remains on your device depends on its purpose and the service that placed it.
