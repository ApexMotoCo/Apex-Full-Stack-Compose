


Apex Moto & Co (“Apex Moto & Co”, “we”, “us” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide to us.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect your personal information when you visit our website, contact us, register for events, purchase products or services, or otherwise interact with Apex Moto & Co.

1. Who We Are

Apex Moto & Co is a UK-based motorcycle community and events organisation.

For the purposes of applicable UK data protection legislation, including the **UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR)** and the **Data Protection Act 2018**, Apex Moto & Co is responsible for the personal information collected through this website and our services.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, please contact us using the details provided on our website.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with Apex Moto & Co, we may collect information including:

* Your name
* Email address
* Telephone number
* Address or location information where necessary
* Motorcycle details, where relevant to an event or service
* Event registration information
* Booking and transaction information
* Information you provide when contacting us
* Social media usernames or profiles where you choose to interact with us
* Photographs or video footage taken at Apex events
* Website usage information, including IP address, browser type and device information
* Cookie and analytics information

We will only collect information that is reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it is being obtained.

3. How We Collect Your Information

We may collect information when you:

* Complete a form on our website
* Register for or attend an Apex Moto & Co event
* Contact us by email, telephone or social media
* Purchase a product or service
* Subscribe to communications from us
* Interact with our website
* Enter a competition or promotion
* Otherwise voluntarily provide information to us

Some information may also be collected automatically when you use our website through cookies and similar technologies.

4. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

* Respond to enquiries
* Process bookings, registrations and purchases
* Organise and administer events
* Communicate important information about events or services
* Provide requested products or services
* Manage our relationship with you
* Improve our website, events and services
* Understand how visitors use our website
* Prevent fraud, misuse or unlawful activity
* Comply with legal and regulatory obligations
* Send marketing communications where you have provided appropriate consent or where otherwise permitted by law

We will not use your personal information for unrelated purposes without a lawful basis for doing so.

5. Lawful Basis for Processing

Under UK data protection law, we must have a lawful basis for processing your personal information.

Depending on the circumstances, we may rely on:

**Consent**
Where you have actively agreed to receive marketing communications or have otherwise given us permission to process your information.

**Contract**
Where processing is necessary to provide a product, service, booking or other agreement you have requested.

**Legal obligation**
Where we are required to process information to comply with the law.

**Legitimate interests**
Where processing is necessary for the legitimate operation and development of Apex Moto & Co, provided that our interests do not override your fundamental privacy rights.

6. Marketing

If you choose to subscribe to marketing communications, we may contact you with information about:

* Apex Moto & Co events
* Motorcycle meets
* New services
* Products and merchandise
* Partnerships and collaborations
* Community updates
* Promotions and other relevant Apex activities

You can unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time by using the unsubscribe option provided in the communication or by contacting us directly.

We will not sell your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

7. Event Photography and Video

Apex Moto & Co events may be photographed or recorded for promotional, marketing and community purposes.

Images or video captured at public or organised Apex events may be used on our website, social media channels, promotional materials and other Apex Moto & Co media.

Where appropriate, we will provide information at events regarding photography and filming.

If you have a specific concern about an image or recording in which you appear, please contact us and we will consider your request in accordance with applicable data protection law.

8. Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies.

Cookies may be used to:

* Make the website function correctly
* Remember preferences
* Understand how visitors use the website
* Measure website performance
* Improve our services
* Support marketing and analytics activities where applicable

Where required by law, we will ask for your consent before placing non-essential cookies on your device.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings and, where applicable, our website cookie controls.

9. Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party providers to help operate Apex Moto & Co and our website.

These may include providers for:

* Website hosting
* Payment processing
* Event registration
* Email communications
* Analytics
* Marketing
* Social media
* Website security

Where third parties process personal information on our behalf, we take reasonable steps to ensure that appropriate data protection and security measures are in place.

Third-party services may have their own privacy policies, and we recommend reviewing them where appropriate.

10. Payments

Where payments are made through our website or an associated third-party platform, payment information may be processed directly by the relevant payment provider.

Apex Moto & Co does not generally need to store complete payment card details.

Payment providers may process your information in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms.

11. How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against:

* Unauthorised access
* Loss
* Misuse
* Alteration
* Disclosure
* Destruction

However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. How Long We Keep Your Information

We will only retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected.

The retention period may depend on:

* The nature of the information
* Why we collected it
* Whether you have an ongoing relationship with us
* Legal or accounting requirements
* Our legitimate business requirements

When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to securely delete or anonymise it.

13. Sharing Your Information

We will not sell or rent your personal information.

We may share information where reasonably necessary with:

* Service providers working on our behalf
* Event partners where necessary to administer an event
* Payment providers
* Website and hosting providers
* Professional advisers
* Law enforcement or regulatory authorities where legally required

We only intend to share information where there is a legitimate reason and appropriate safeguards are in place.

14. Your Data Protection Rights

Under UK data protection law, you may have the right to:

* Request access to the personal information we hold about you
* Request correction of inaccurate information
* Request deletion of your information in certain circumstances
* Request restriction of processing in certain circumstances
* Object to certain processing
* Request transfer of certain information to another organisation
* Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
* Complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)

You can exercise your rights by contacting Apex Moto & Co using the contact details provided on our website.

We may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests.

15. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent or involvement from a parent or guardian where required by law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us inappropriately, please contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

16. International Data Transfers

Some third-party service providers we use may process information outside the United Kingdom.

Where this occurs, we will take reasonable steps to ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place in accordance with applicable UK data protection law.
